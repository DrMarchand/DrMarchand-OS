# Flywheel

Status: Canonical concept
Registry path: `registry/concepts/flywheel.md`
System: DrMarchand-OS™

---

## Definition

The Flywheel is the motion, continuity, and redirection layer of DrMarchand-OS™.

It allows events, state changes, and execution paths to redirect without forcing every component to directly depend on every other component.

---

## Role

The Flywheel supports:

- system momentum
- route redirection
- continuity between states
- controlled handoff between components
- non-destructive movement through the architecture

---

## Relationship

```text
Registry = meaning
Atlas = map
Flywheel = motion
Workbench = preparation
Engine = compilation
```

The Flywheel does not define what concepts mean. It moves and redirects paths according to Registry-defined meaning and Atlas-defined topology.

---

## Boundary

The Flywheel is not a database, brand, or generic animation metaphor.

It is a governed architectural layer for motion and continuity inside DrMarchand-OS™.
