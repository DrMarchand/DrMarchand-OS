# Atlas

Status: Canonical concept
Registry path: `registry/concepts/atlas.md`
System: DrMarchand-OS™

---

## Definition

Atlas is the map layer.

Atlas defines nodes, relationships, coordinates, references, navigation paths, and system topology. Atlas makes the architecture navigable.

---

## Role

Atlas is responsible for:

- mapping system entities
- tracking relationships
- identifying coordinates
- supporting navigation
- exposing topology
- helping bridge interfaces understand where they connect

---

## Relationship

```text
Registry defines meaning
Atlas maps relationships
Flywheel moves execution paths
Workbench prepares working forms
Engine compiles executable definitions
```

Atlas depends on Registry meaning. It should not invent definitions independently.

---

## Boundary

Atlas should not be overloaded with business logic, authority decisions, or external platform ownership.

Atlas maps. It does not own the system.
