# Workbench

Status: Canonical concept
Registry path: `registry/concepts/workbench.md`
System: DrMarchand-OS™

---

## Definition

Workbench is the preparation layer where working forms are reviewed, shaped, tested, validated, packaged, or prepared before release or preservation.

There is no single generic Workbench.

---

## Canonical Distinction

```text
📚 DrMarchand's ⚛︎ Library™ → 🪑 DrMarchand's ⎈ Workbench™
🔬 DrMarchand's Lab⚛︎ratory™ → ⑁ DrMarchand's ⎈ Workbench™
```

The Library Workbench handles knowledge review, curation, publication preparation, and recall.

The Laboratory Workbench handles build, prototype, compile, test, validate, package, and release.

---

## Role

Workbench supports controlled preparation before a thing becomes canonical, released, preserved, or bridged.

---

## Boundary

Workbench is not the final archive and not the public release surface.

It is a governed preparation layer. Final meaning belongs to the Registry. Final preservation belongs to the proper archival authority.
