# UNI

Status: Canonical concept
Registry path: `registry/concepts/uni.md`
System: DrMarchand-OS™

---

## Definition

UNI means one.

UNI is the foundational gear logic that allows DrMarchand-OS™ to operate as one governed system instead of a loose pile of parts.

UNI is not primarily a brand, license, or contract at this stage. It is engine-facing foundational logic.

---

## Role

UNI enables the system to preserve unity across separate layers:

```text
Atlas maps
Flywheel redirects
Workbench prepares
Engine compiles
Registry remembers
Orchard grows
```

---

## Behavior

UNI should be treated as:

- modular
- closable
- engine-facing
- foundational
- relationship-aware
- Registry-governed

---

## Boundary

UNI should not be used as a vague synonym for the whole ecosystem.

UNI is the foundational gear logic inside DrMarchand-OS™. It supports the system; it does not replace the Registry, Atlas, Flywheel, Workbench, or DrMarchand's ⚙︎ Nɛuro-Forge Engine™.
